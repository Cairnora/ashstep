Delete your account

You can permanently delete your Ashstep account in the app or request help if you can no longer sign in.

1. Delete in the app

  1. Sign in to Ashstep.
  2. Open Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account.
  3. Review the warning, confirm the request, and complete the final confirmation.

This is the fastest way to verify ownership and delete the account. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

2. If you cannot sign in

Email contact@akoracorp.com from the address connected to your account when possible, with the subject “Account deletion request.” Never send a password or sign-in code. We may request limited information to verify ownership before acting.

3. What is deleted

The deletion flow is designed to remove your authentication account, profile, optional profile images, challenges and check-ins, pledges and emotions, craving-support sessions, timeline and weekly-report records, Sage messages, notification settings and devices, and other app records tied to your account.

4. Limited retention

Some data may remain temporarily in backups, security or abuse-prevention logs, transaction records, or records that law requires us to keep. Those records are restricted, are not used to continue the deleted account, and are removed or de-identified under the applicable retention cycle.

5. Store subscriptions

Deleting Ashstep does not automatically cancel a subscription managed by Apple or Google. If you have a store subscription, cancel it separately in the relevant store settings.
