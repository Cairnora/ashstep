Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Ashstep Quit Smoking collects, why it is used, who helps process it, and the choices available to you.

Effective August 29, 2026

1. Information we process

Depending on the features you use, Ashstep may process account details, an optional profile photo, smoking-profile answers, goals, check-ins, pledges, emotions, craving-support records, timeline and weekly-report records, Sage messages, notification settings, support requests, and technical information needed to operate and secure the app.

App-improvement events include safe screen names, feature actions, and app language, but not the text of your journal-style entries. You can turn this collection off in General settings.

2. How information is used

We use information to sign you in, save and synchronize progress, provide requested features, deliver enabled notifications, answer support requests, protect the service, diagnose problems, improve the app, and meet legal obligations. Ashstep does not use your entries to diagnose or treat a disease.

3. Health-related information

Some entries may reveal health-related information. Ashstep asks for separate consent before creating these core records. You may withdraw consent by deleting your account or contacting us.

4. Service providers and sharing

Supabase supports authentication and data storage. Google and Apple may process sign-in information, Google Firebase Analytics supports app-improvement measurement, and Expo, Apple, and Google support notifications. These providers may process information outside your country under their own privacy policies. If you make an app-store purchase, Apple or Google processes the transaction.

We do not sell personal data or provide it to advertising networks. We share information only with providers needed to run Ashstep, when you request it, when required by law, or when reasonably necessary to protect users and the service.

5. Retention and deletion

Account information is generally kept while your account is active or as needed to provide Ashstep. Account deletion removes the authentication account and associated app records. Limited records may remain temporarily in backups, security systems, transaction records, or where law requires retention.

6. Your choices

You can update profile and notification settings, export supported data, revoke device permissions, turn off app-improvement data, and delete your account. You may also request access, correction, deletion, or another right available under local law. We may need to verify account ownership.

7. Children, security, changes, and contact

Ashstep is not designed for children. Where local law requires it, a parent or legal guardian must consent. We use access controls, encrypted connections, and provider security features, but no service can guarantee absolute security.

We may update this policy and will revise the effective date when we do. Ashstep is operated by AKORA Co., Ltd. For privacy questions or requests, email contact@akoracorp.com. Do not send passwords or sign-in codes.
