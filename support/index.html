Support

Use this page for app, account, privacy, and deletion help. Support is provided by email.

App help

For sign-in, syncing, notifications, challenges, or another app issue, email contact@akoracorp.com. Include your platform, app version, what you expected, and what happened. Screenshots are helpful when they do not reveal private information.

Never send your password, sign-in code, recovery key, full payment details, or more sensitive information than is needed.

Account and privacy requests

Use Settings in the app to update your profile, notification preferences, and supported data export. For access, correction, consent withdrawal, or another privacy request, contact us from the email connected to your account when possible. We may ask for limited information to verify account ownership.

Account deletion

Signed-in users can choose Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account. If you cannot sign in, follow the account-deletion instructions and email us. Deletion is permanent after the confirmation flow completes.

Health and safety

Ashstep support cannot diagnose symptoms, recommend medication, or provide emergency care. Contact a qualified healthcare professional for medical questions. If you may be in immediate danger or have severe or concerning symptoms, contact local emergency services now.

Response and operator details

We aim to review support messages as reasonably practical but do not guarantee an immediate response.
